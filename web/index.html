How it's made
1️⃣  Research a surprising true story with a visual cause & effect
2️⃣  ~60 s script — hook, escalation, snap moment, one physics idea
3️⃣  Voice-over timed word-by-word; shot plan in code (story.ts)
4️⃣  three.js world + ink outlines + painted textures + lens blur, inside Remotion
5️⃣  Synthesized march score & SFX (no stock audio), mixed to −15 LUFS
